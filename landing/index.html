Features

Everything your money needs.

Built for people who want financial clarity without the spreadsheet grind.

🔔
Auto Expense Logging
AiCoinTrack reads M-Pesa SMS, bank alerts, and Base blockchain events — every transaction logged the moment it happens. No manual entry ever.
🤖
AI Categorization
Hugging Face zero-shot classification categorizes every transaction instantly. 94%+ accuracy. Learns your habits and improves over time.
⛓️
Onchain Storage
Every expense is optionally stored on Base L2 — transparent, verifiable, and permanently yours. Your financial history, on the blockchain.
🛒
Smart Shopping Lists
Build shopping lists linked to your budget categories. Get real-time budget health warnings. Checkout converts items directly to expenses.
🎯
Savings Goals
Set goals, track progress, and let AiCoinTrack auto-sweep your monthly surplus. Emergency fund, vacation, gadget — all in one place.
📊
Zero-Based Budgeting
Use the 50/30/20 rule or build your own. Every shilling has a job. Real-time variance tracking shows planned vs actual — always.
💰
Invisible Wallets
No seed phrases. No gas fees. No crypto confusion. Smart accounts created silently — users see KES balances, blockchain does the work underneath.
🔵
Base Miniapp
Access AiCoinTrack directly in the Base app. No install needed. Track onchain transactions and log expenses manually — lightweight and always ready.
How It Works

Up and running in minutes.

Get started in minutes with our simple onboarding process.

1
Connect Your Accounts
Link your M-Pesa, email notifications, and Base wallet. AiCoinTrack handles the rest — no manual setup per transaction.
2
Transactions Auto-Log
Every payment — M-Pesa, bank, onchain — is captured automatically. AI categorizes it in real time and stores it in your budget.
3
Get AI Insights
Your dashboard surfaces spending patterns, budget warnings, and AI recommendations — so you always know where your money is going and why.
4
Hit Your Goals
Monthly closeouts sweep surplus to savings. Progress bars keep you on track. Your financial life, finally automated.
